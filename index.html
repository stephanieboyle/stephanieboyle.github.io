About Me

I started out in neuroscience research, then moved into teaching, product analytics, and analytics leadership. Although the domain changes, the things I love most stay the same: exploring data, building and shipping good products, leading teams, and helping folk grow.

These days, I’m Reporting & Analytics Lead at SSEN Transmission. Before that, I worked in product analytics at komoot and led a team of data instructors at CodeClan.

I’m particularly interested in mission-driven businesses, where I can see how the work contributes to something I care about.

Outside work, you’ll find me hiking, mountain biking, or bikepacking. Give me a trail and a reason to spend the day outdoors, and I’m happy.
