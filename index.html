ANALYTICS LEADER · HIGHLANDS, SCOTLAND

Hi, I’m Steph.

I’m a technical analytics leader with a PhD and over 10 years’ experience across product analytics, analytics engineering and strategy.

I use data and experimentation to understand user behaviour, shape product development and measure whether changes make a difference. As a leader, I focus on creating happy, inclusive and motivated teams where people are supported to do their best work.

I’m based in the Scottish Highlands, and you’ll usually find me outside on the trails with my dogs!

A little more about me ↘

About Me

Currently, I lead a team of eight across data science, analytics engineering, and BI at SSEN Transmission. I’ve built the team and reporting service from scratch, and we’re working towards a fully supported self-service model that allows the business to achieve its digital and net zero ambitions.

Before SSE, I was a Senior Product Analyst at komoot, working alongside product managers, designers, and engineers to explore how people used the app and where we could make it better. I joined as one of the very first product analysts, and my work covered growth, engagement, retention, and conversion, bringing data into decisions about what to build and improve.

Before komoot, my work was a real mix. I designed and taught a data analytics bootcamp at CodeClan, covering Python, SQL, R, statistics, and machine learning, and grew and led the instructor team there. I also worked in a pharmaceutical data science scale-up, doing analytics for clinical trials. And I spent four years completing a PhD in computational neuroscience, using machine learning to build predictive models of how the brain combines information from different senses.

Whatever the company or domain, I enjoy both the technical detail and the people side of the work: digging into an analysis, figuring out how to build something, and helping a team work through a difficult question. I’m drawn to roles where I can keep doing both.
